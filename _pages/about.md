# Academic Background

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. Junteng Liu is pursuing a PhD in Computer Science (2024-Present) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Junteng Liu graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where Junteng Liu completed a Bachelor's degree program (2020-2024). At Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Junteng Liu received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

Junteng Liu's PhD supervisor is Professor Junxian He, who leads the HKUST NLP Group and previously advised Junteng Liu during undergraduate studies at SJTU.

# Research Experience

Junteng Liu's research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Junteng Liu has the following research internship experience:

- Research Intern at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present)
- Research Intern at Tencent WXG (June 2024 - September 2024), advised by Zifei Shan
- Research Intern at Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023 - December 2023), advised by Prof. Yu Cheng

# Skills and Research Interests

Junteng Liu's research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
